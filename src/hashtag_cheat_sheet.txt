Kokomo Arts Hashtag Cheat Sheet

Always include:
- 2–3 location tags (#KokomoIndiana, #DowntownKokomo)
- 2 arts tags (#KokomoArts, #IndianaArts)
- 1 brand tag (#KokomoArtAssociation, #ArtworksGallery, or #KokomoArtCenter)

Avoid using more than ONE of these together:
#KokomoIndiana / #KokomoIN / #ThisIsKokomo

Tag partners, artists, and venues using @ mentions whenever possible.
